Undergraduate thesis, Cadence Virtuoso, 90 nm GPDK.

Thesis

Delay optimization of an 8-bit arithmetic and logic unit using the full-swing gate diffusion input technique.

The primary focus of this work was reducing the delay of an 8-bit arithmetic and logic unit (ALU) using the full-swing gate diffusion input (FS-GDI) technique. FS-GDI was chosen over CMOS for its higher efficiency and lower power consumption.

Rather than using complete ICs such as an adder or a 1-bit ALU, the design was assembled from building blocks of individual logic gates — AND, OR, and so on. Once every required circuit was built, the blocks were connected to form the full 8-bit ALU.

Cadence Virtuoso was the simulation environment, and 90-nanometer GPDK technology set the transistor sizing.
